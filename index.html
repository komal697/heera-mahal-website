About Heera Mahal

A Venue Like No Other

Welcome to Heera Mahal, where timeless elegance meets modern luxury. Nestled in the heart of Jaipur, Heera Mahal is more than just a marriage garden — it's a regal venue designed to transform your dream events into unforgettable memories. From lavish weddings and receptions to intimate engagements and cultural festivities, Heera Mahal offers a picturesque setting, world-class amenities, and a team dedicated to perfection. Our majestic architecture, sprawling green lawns, luxurious décor, and seamless event services make us one of the most sought-after wedding venues in Jaipur.
